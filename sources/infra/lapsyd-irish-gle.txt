Language name : Irish, Donegal dialect
ISO 639-3     : gle
Alternate     : Gaeilge
Classification: Indo-European, Celtic, Goidelic
Location      : Ireland, especially in northwestern parts of the island.

=== CONSONANT NOTES ===
The consonant system shown is that favored by Ni Chasaide (1999), which omits voiceless nasals and liquids — these being treated as sequences with /h/.

=== VOWEL NOTES ===
"Vowels fall into long and short sets ... long and short pairs of vowels are qualitatively distinct" (Ni Chasaide 1999). She exemplifies 5 short and 6 long vowels, plus three diphthongs. Because of the differences in quality both the long and short vowels are treated as basic. In addition a schwa is shown on the vowel chart: this appears in a high proportion of the unstressed vowels in the transcribed passage, but is regarded as a reduced variant of other vowels. Other dialects are reported to have nasalized vowels.
