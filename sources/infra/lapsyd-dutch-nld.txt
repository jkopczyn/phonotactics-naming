Language name : Dutch
ISO 639-3     : nld
Alternate     : Nederlands
Classification: Indo-European, Germanic, West
Location      : The Netherlands and generally northern parts of Belgium

COUNTS: consonants=18 basic vowels=13 total vowels=16 C/V ratio=1.125 top C=t top V=

=== SYLLABLE STRUCTURE ===
Syllcat (cf. WALS ch.12): Complex
Syllabic index (O+N+C) = 8   [Onset=3 Nucleus=2 Coda=3]
Canonical Form      : (C)(C)(C)V(V)(C)(C)(C)(C)
Syllabic Restriction: -
Syllabic notes      : (C)(C)(C)V(V)(C)(C)(C)(C)

=== STRESS ===
Stresscat (cf. WALS ch.14/16): Lexical
Fixed Stress: No   Weight-sensitivity: Coda
Stress notes: Stress location is unpredictable but there are few minimal pairs.

=== TONE ===
Tonecat: None
Tone notes: None, at least in Standard Dutch

=== CONSONANT NOTES ===
Basic coronals are said to be laminal except for /|/.

=== VOWEL NOTES ===
Gussenhoven '99 positions the "lax" front vowels on the vowel chart as if they were central but uses front vowel symbols. Three vowels transcribed as long /e P o / are said to be "narrow diphthongs" /ei Py ou/ and are added to dipthong list here. Gussenhoven '99 recognizes several additional long vowels in "recent loans" not included here.
