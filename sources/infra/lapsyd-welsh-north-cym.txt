Language name : Welsh, Northern
ISO 639-3     : cym
Alternate     : Cymraeg
Classification: Indo-European, Celtic, Brythonic
Location      : Primarily in the North and West of Wales (Dyfed and Gwynedd) (point location is Bangor)

COUNTS: consonants=23 basic vowels=7 total vowels=27 C/V ratio=0.85185185185185 top C= top V=

=== SYLLABLE STRUCTURE ===
Syllcat (cf. WALS ch.12): Complex
Syllabic index (O+N+C) = 7   [Onset=3 Nucleus=2 Coda=2]
Canonical Form      : (C)(C)(C)V(V)(C)(C)
Syllabic Restriction: C)(C)(C)V(V)(C)(C)
Syllabic notes      : (C)(C)(C)V(V)(C)(C) e.g. /sgləin/ "shine", /tluːs/ "pretty", /ɬuŋk/ "lip", /gwevl/ "lip"

=== STRESS ===
Stresscat (cf. WALS ch.14/16): Lexical
Fixed Stress: Penult   Weight-sensitivity: None
Stress notes: "Primary stress, in indigenous Welsh words, falls normally on the penultimate syllable, but in some cases on the ultimate" (Thomas 1992: 331).

=== TONE ===
Tonecat: None
Tone notes: None

=== CONSONANT NOTES ===
The 'mutated' nasals written are considered unitary consonants by some but are analyzed as Nh sequences here following Ball & Williams (2001). The two affricates /tʃ, dʒ/ occur in more recent English loans, but are not included here.

=== VOWEL NOTES ===
Short mid vowels /e, o/ are somewhat lower than their long counterparts.
